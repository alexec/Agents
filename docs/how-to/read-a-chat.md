# Read a chat

This page tells you where a chat opens, how to follow the agent's plan at the top of the
chat, and how to see the agent's thoughts. The Mac, iPhone, iPad and the web page work
the same way.

## Where a chat opens

A chat always opens at its latest message. The app keeps the chat hidden until it is in
place, so the first thing you see is the end, with no scroll. The end is what the agent
did last, so it is the part you usually came to read.

- **Go back to the end.** When you scroll up, a down arrow shows at the foot of the chat.
  Click or tap it to go to the end.
- **On the Mac,** choose **View ▸ Jump to Latest** (Command-Down Arrow).
- **On the web page,** press Command-Down Arrow or End while the prompt does not have
  the keyboard.
- **Click or tap the session again** in the sidebar, when it is already open. The chat
  goes to its end.

A question or a permission card shows where the agent asked it, not after the whole
chat. When the agent asked before its last message, scroll up to find the card. The
session's row in the sidebar is under **Needs You** until you answer. See
[Answer a question or a permission request](answer-a-question.md).

## Follow the plan

When the agent makes a plan, a card shows at the top of the chat. It stays there while
you scroll, so you can see how far the agent is without reading the whole chat.

The card shows:

- the step the agent works on now, or **Plan** between steps;
- how many steps are done, such as **2 of 5** (**2/5** on a narrow iPhone or iPad screen);
- a thin bar that fills as steps are done.

Click or tap the card to see every step. Click or tap it again to fold it. The step in
progress is in bold.

The card shows only the plan in force. When the agent drops a plan, the card goes. The
dropped plan stays in the chat where the agent dropped it, struck through, with
**The agent dropped this plan**.

## Show the agent's thoughts

Most runtimes send the agent's thinking as well as its replies. The chat does not show
these thoughts at first, because most people want to read what the agent did, not how
it got there.

To see them, choose **Show Thoughts**. Choose **Hide Thoughts** to leave them out again.

- **On the Mac:** choose **View ▸ Show Thoughts**.
- **On iPhone or iPad:** in a chat, tap **···** at the top, then **Show Thoughts**.
- **On the web page:** in a chat, click **···** at the top, then **Show Thoughts**.

With the thoughts shown, each thought is one line that starts **Thought:**, with its
first words. Click or tap the line to read the whole thought. With them hidden, the chat, the
turns you open and the steps of a subagent show no thoughts.

Each device keeps its own choice. Turning it on in one browser does not turn it on for
the Mac or your iPhone.
