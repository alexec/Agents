# See what needs you from your Home screen

A widget on your Home screen shows how many agent sessions are waiting on you, and the
newest few of them by name. Tap the number to go to the first project with something
waiting, or a row to go straight to that conversation.

The number is the one the Mac's Dock badge shows: every agent asking a question, waiting
for a permission, or stopped with something it could not finish.

## Before you start

- Agents on your Mac, and the iPhone or iPad app installed and paired with it (see
  [Answer a question or a permission request](answer-a-question.md)).

## Steps

1. On your iPhone or iPad, press and hold an empty part of the Home screen, tap **Edit**,
   then **Add Widget**.
2. Search for **Agents** and tap it.
3. Choose the size: **Small** for the number, **Medium** for the number and the sessions
   behind it. Tap **Add Widget**, then **Done**.

The widget fills in as soon as the app has been open since you installed it. Until then it
says **Open Agents**, because it has nothing to report and a zero would be a claim it
cannot back.

**Read it**

- **Small** is the number, and whether anybody is waiting. Orange means somebody is.
- **Medium** adds up to four sessions, newest first: what the agent is called, which
  project it is in, and what it is asking for. If more are waiting than fit, **+3 more**
  counts the rest.
- Once nothing is waiting, the widget says **Nothing waiting** rather than showing a zero
  with nothing under it.

**Open one**

- Tap a row to go to that conversation.
- Tap the number, or the widget when there is no row to tap, to go to the first project
  with something waiting. When nothing is waiting, it goes to the list of projects.

**Keep it honest**

The number is written by the app, so it is only as fresh as the app last knew. The app
writes it whenever the count moves, when it comes to the front, and when your Mac tells it
something changed, so a number that has not moved for a while stays as it was until you
open the app. When it is more than a few minutes old, the widget says how long ago it was
updated underneath.

## If it doesn't work

- **The widget says Open Agents.** The app has not been opened on this device yet, or the
  count has never been written here. Open the app once.
- **The number is one or more behind the Mac.** The app has not heard from your Mac. Open
  it: it refreshes, and the widget follows.
- **Nothing is waiting but the Mac's Dock badge says otherwise.** An agent that has
  finished and that you have not looked at is counted until you read it. Read it in the
  app, and the count goes down.
- **The widget is greyed out.** That is a placeholder, which iOS draws before the first
  real one has been made. It draws what the widget will look like, and is never counted.
