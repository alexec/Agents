## Summary

Twenty-three issues were filed, **#200–#222**, all labelled `bug`:
- **Critical:** 1 (#200)
- **High:** 8 (#201–#208)
- **Medium:** 13 (#209–#221)
- **Low:** 1 (#222)

The top three:

1. **One request can kill the host (#200).** `agents/turns` with `limit: -1` traps on a Swift range, and nothing clamps it. **Reproduced:** the scratch agentsd died within two seconds of one call. Any paired client (web page, phone, peer) can send it, and every agent on the host stops. At the other end of the same gap, there is no ceiling: clients fetch a whole turn in one call, and a lead's single turn can be a 53 MB transcript.
2. **The daemon's front door can shut for good (#201).** The accept thread `return`s on its first error other than `EINTR` (`DaemonServer.swift:204-209`). One descriptor spike (`EMFILE`) or an `ECONNABORTED` leaves agentsd alive at 0 % CPU and unreachable, with no log line. That matches the "app does nothing, agentsd alive" incident of 2026-09-20. The two other accept loops spin a core on the same errors instead.
3. **What the host sends and starts is not bounded by what anyone is looking at (#202, #203).**
   - Every agent change broadcasts the whole record to every client. **Measured: 20.9 KB per label toggle.** Every agent's `agent/entry` and terminal output also goes to every client, watched or not, and the phone decodes it on the main thread.
   - Prewarm (#183) ignores the pool's cap until each launch finishes. **Measured: 12 chats opened started 12 runtimes at once** against a cap of 3, where each real adapter is about 0.5 GB.

## The fix direction: bound the data

As on 2026-10-03, the fixes should bound what is held, sent and started, not add concurrency.
- **Notifications (#203):**
  - lean `agent/changed`;
  - `agent/entry` and terminal output only to connections watching that agent;
  - archived changes not re-filed by clients that don't hold them.
- **Requests (#200, #210):**
  - clamp every `limit` and `before`;
  - page inside a turn;
  - read sessions from the end;
  - limit `list_sessions`;
  - an LRU instead of clear-at-16 caches.
- **Starts (#202):** prewarms count against the cap while they launch.
- **Per-project work (#204, #216, #218):**
  - compute the folder set once, incrementally;
  - cache tiles, pins and workflow digests, invalidated by the project watch;
  - hold workflow records in memory.
- **Git (#207):**
  - deadlines on every process;
  - statuses only for what is shown, cached, with `GIT_OPTIONAL_LOCKS=0`.
- **Clients (#213, #214, #215):** let go of what a page fetched when it closes; per-project and per-agent observed cells; bound raw entries rather than rows.

## Checked and found sound

- **#164's tallies:** they cannot drift. `AgentTable`'s counts move only through its subscript, and `ProjectTallyTests` checks them against a full rebuild over 600 random changes.
- **#165:** a folded fold reads nothing; one change moves one element of one project's shelf; `agentTitles` sits behind a revision.
- **#167:** 8 MB and 32 MB outbound caps with a 10 s write deadline; `BoundedLines`; one fan-out frame per uplink; top-level `answeredID`.
- **#168:** the `hostApp` role reads four methods and hears nothing. The stranger log is bounded at 256 keys.
- **#169:** approvals fail closed, the file is left in place, and the first Approve recovers. The edges are in #205.
- **#172:** jitter everywhere, `SOMAXCONN`, `hostChanged` said once, and `PendingPosts` capped at 200.
- **#173 (macOS):** one watch per project, with worktrees and `.git/objects` excluded.
- **#175:**
  - catch-up is single-flight and bounded to what is on screen;
  - folder watches are released;
  - the picture LRU;
  - `remote.log` is capped at 2 × 256 KB on its own queue.
- **#177:** `DaemonLog` keeps one handle at 2 × 4 MiB; spend is held in memory; `events.jsonl` lines are whole; `archive.json` is written only on change.
- **#183's pool, once launched:** cap 0–8 (default 3), a 30-minute ceiling, a ticker that stops when empty, side maps bounded and dropped on archive, and warm runtimes ended on shutdown.
- **Leases:** small, written whole, timers only while held.
- **Event waits:** indexed (except the deadline walk, #218).
- **Dashboard history:** compacted to about 250 points per tile, 8 MB per project, 60 tiles.
- **Pins:** capped at 10, with stamped reads.
- **Disk full, 2026-10-03:** no file was torn or truncated. Every whole-file store recovered on its next write, and `ProjectConfig` refuses to write over a file it can't parse.
- **The web page:** link timers, listeners and intervals are cleared (apart from those in #214); events and archived pages are capped; nothing grows in localStorage or IndexedDB.
- **Warm on intent from the clients:** at most one request per agent and reason every 15 s, not per keystroke.
