I have been running several coding agents at once for most of this year, and I spent too much
time finding out which one was stuck.

So I built an app for it. Agents (in the App Store as Loops: Agent Workflows) runs the coding
agents you already have, such as Claude, Codex and Copilot, and sorts them by who needs you.
Agents take turns with the simulator, wait for each other, and keep going when you close the
window. You answer them from your iPhone, your iPad or a browser.

Its source is on GitHub for anyone to read, and it is open to everyone today.
