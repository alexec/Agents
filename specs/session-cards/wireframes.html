Session cards

Every card, strip, banner and tile drawn inside one session's chat, in the order a person meets them from the top of the chat to the prompt bar. Drawn from the web client (Web/src/views/Chat.tsx) with the real design tokens; the Mac window and the Remote draw the same set from Shared/UI, with the differences listed in section H.

Count: 40 kinds in 7 places, plus 7 variants of the question card. The blue chips name the family each card belongs to; the families are the start point for making them simpler.

What stands out (for the simpler set)

  • Eight families, many recipes. "Attention" is drawn three ways (border, border + wash, title only); a "note" is drawn three ways (tinted box, well box, no box).
  • Asks live in two places. Sandbox failure (15), policy stop (16) and the view's Show (19) ask the person from inside the transcript; every other ask is in "Waiting for you".
  • "Waiting for you" holds things that are not asks: the live tile (29).
  • Leases are said twice: the lease line (25) and the lease wait card (33l).
  • Progress is said twice: the plan strip (7) and the plan view in the transcript (14).
  • Six top banners can stack (1–5, plus cost limit at the bottom), each with its own buttons.
  • The clients do not agree on the look. The policy stop, account move and archive cards each have a different tint per client. A shown file is a card (18) on two clients and a strip (H2) on the phone.
  • Bugs seen: the confirm card (28g) does not show its question on the web; the account move strip (4) has no style.
