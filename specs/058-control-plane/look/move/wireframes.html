R · 3. Tell everyone, and who knows
Announce gives every window, phone and server the new address, beside this one. Those online learn it within seconds; the list fills as they do. The person moves when they like: anyone still to hear learns it later from this Mac, which keeps forwarding. A build too old to follow is named, with what to do. Withdraw takes the new address back.
