Govern agents with policies

One policy says what agents may do. It has layers: Organisation → Group → You → Folder. Each layer can only make a rule tighter. The tightest value applies. Every value shows the layer that sets it.

Settings gets a new group, Agents. Its Policy pane is the editor for your own layer. The rules that are settings in five places today move into it.
