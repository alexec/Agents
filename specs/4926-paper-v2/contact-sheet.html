Paper v2

The design system of Agents on the Mac, iPhone, iPad and the web. Quiet, clear and warm: charcoal on paper, one face, thin icons, and edges and shading that you feel more than see.
#4926 · proposal ·

Colour

Few colours, each with one job. Surfaces are warm greys; text is charcoal; colour means something (a link, a state, a label) and is never decoration. Each chip shows light, then dark. Every text colour is 4.5:1 or more on the surfaces it is drawn on.

Type

One face for everything you read, and its mono for code: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Mono (Braille Institute, OFL). Letters that look alike are drawn to differ, which helps people with low vision and dyslexia. Sizes follow the person's text size on every platform.

Edges and depth

Very subtle. A hairline at 1.2:1 separates; a shadow you barely see lifts. Only a sheet casts a shadow you notice. A control's own edge is stronger (3:1), so it can be found. Focus is a soft cobalt ring.

Icons

Lucide (ISC), one set on all three clients, drawn at a 1.5 stroke: thin, round, quiet. 16 in a row, 18 in a control, 22 here. An icon is ink unless it shows a state. Then it takes that state's colour, and a word always goes with it.
