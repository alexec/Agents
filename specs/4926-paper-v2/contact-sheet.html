Paper v2

The design system of Agents on the Mac, iPhone, iPad and the web. Clear and crisp: deep charcoal on paper, one face, thin icons. Lines set things apart, not shadows. Flat paper and lines. The one gradient is on the large grey column, the sidebar, where it gives the page depth without a shadow.
#4926 · proposal ·

Colour

Few colours, each with one job, at high contrast. Surfaces are near white; text is deep charcoal (17:1); colour means something (a link, a state, a label) and is never decoration. Each chip shows light, then dark. Every text colour is 4.5:1 or more on every surface, the selection included.

Type

One face for everything you read, and its mono for code: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Mono (Braille Institute, OFL). Letters that look alike are drawn to differ, which helps people with low vision and dyslexia. Sizes follow the person's text size on every platform.

Edges and depth

Flat and crisp. A line (1.5:1) sets things apart; nothing on the page casts a shadow. Only what floats over the page (a menu, a toast, a sheet) has one, short and close. A control's edge is 3:1 or more. One gradient only: the large grey background, the sidebar, goes from paper to a slightly deeper grey, top to bottom. Never on a coloured background, and never on a small thing such as a button, a chip, a badge or a notice: those are flat paper with a line. Colour never fills: it is in text, icons, a line (a state's edge, the selection's bar) and a switch that is on. Focus is a cobalt ring.

Icons

Lucide (ISC), one set on all three clients, drawn at a 1.5 stroke: thin, round, quiet. 16 in a row, 18 in a control, 22 here. An icon is ink unless it shows a state. Then it takes that state's colour, and a word always goes with it.
