C · Mac: what a subagent is doing, in the sidebar
Once the app opts in, a subagent's own steps and words arrive under a session of their own, not the agent's, and the Agent tool card is no longer sent. Unrouted, the subagent's report would read as the agent's. Proposed: they go into the subagent's own page, opened by Steps (or the card in the transcript), in the sidebar where Changes and Terminal are. The chat shows only what the agent itself said and did. A permission the subagent asks for is still asked in the chat, named as the subagent's.
