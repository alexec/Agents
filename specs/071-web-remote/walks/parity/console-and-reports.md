# Parity: Console and Reports (#1804)

A screen of [the parity table](../parity.md), which says what the paths and verdicts mean.

The control plane's two apps beside the Workspace, on the web only: the Console (admins) and Reports (scoped by role). The Mac and the Remote draw the Me scope of Reports in Cost ▸ Reports (Spend, Outcomes, Time, Runtimes, Adoption and Workflows, #2183) and leave the group and organisation scopes and the Console to the web.
