# Faces, taken further

Six treatments of the same idea. `faces-sheet.png` shows them together and at 32 and 16
points, which is the size that decides it.

| | Treatment | At 16 points |
|---|---|---|
| A | Graded, as first drawn | Fails. The greys merge and the eyes go |
| B | Even weight, bigger heads, tighter grid | Holds. Four things, clearly four |
| C | One working, three not: what the app looks like most of the time | Holds, and still says one of four |
| D | Three in formation | Holds best, but it is no longer a grid |
| E | Outlined | Fails. The line thickens until the heads fill in |
| F | A crowd, one in front | Fails differently: it stops being a group and starts looking like one animal |

The eyes are what carry the idea and the first thing lost when the mark shrinks, so the
heads have to be as large and as few as the idea allows. B is that, without changing what
was chosen.
